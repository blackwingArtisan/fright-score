## Post 1: the hook

Hour 60 of a horror jam and the door still needs a scare.

Fright Score writes an original horror cue, a recreated Sega Genesis FM chip performs it, and the sting lands
on the exact second you ask for. Free, in your browser.

## Post 2: the Frankenstein

The AI writes the sheet music, never the sound.

A score director plans the cue (key, tempo, sections, the second the scare lands). A YM2612 FM chip
recreation plays it. Export the same cue as a .vgm of real Genesis register writes.

https://fright-score.pages.dev #Kiroween #chiptune #SegaGenesis

## Post 3: on the event, not the timeline

Games don't know when the player opens the door.

Every Fright Score sting sits at the end of a planned 0.6 s silence, so the hit lands 0.6 s after the event.
Measured: 0.598 s in Godot. Try the door in the browser:

https://fright-score.pages.dev #gamedev #godotengine #Kiroween

## Post 4: built with Kiro

Kiro specced it, built it, and now calls it as a tool.

EARS specs, steering, hooks, a scoped sound-design agent, and Fright Score's own MCP server, called from a Kiro
session with the hash matching the README. Kiro then scored a game level and this project's video soundtrack
through it. Every session and credit is logged:

https://github.com/blackwingArtisan/fright-score #Kiro #Kiroween #MCP
