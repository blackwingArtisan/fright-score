How to read it

Square pads are modulators, round pads are carriers, and copper traces show the algorithm that wires them. Pads glow with the envelope levels the renderer recorded for every frame of the cue, so you can watch the machine breathe.

    The scare lands on the event
    
          
    A game does not know the second a player will open a door. Here the loop cuts to silence when you do, and the hit lands 0.6 s later, out of the quiet. Both sounds are rendered cues played back from files, the same two files the Godot example plays. Only the keyboard in this app is synthesized in real time.
    
        
    
        
    
          
          
          
        
    
        
    The door is locked until you enter the hallway. Press E while this scene has focus to open it.
    
        
        
    Enter the hallway, then open the door to measure the scare.
    
        
    The figure is the audio clock's schedule: the time from the door event to the hit as the browser placed it. It does not include the delay of your speakers or headphones.
