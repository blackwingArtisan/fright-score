FS-666 channels

Each row is one instrument. Square pads are modulators, round pads are carriers; copper traces show the algorithm. Pads glow with the envelope levels the renderer recorded for every frame of the cue, so you can watch the machine breathe.

Play the chip live

Real-time FM synthesis on the audio thread (an AudioWorklet): the same operators and envelopes that render the cue, running sample by sample as you play. Keys A to K, Z and X change octave, or tap.
